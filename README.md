# My Favorite Series of All Time
## The Sopranos

<h2>Information about the series</h2>
<body> The Sopranos is really about Tony Soprano, a New Jersey mob boss who’s constantly trying to keep his life from falling apart. On one side, he’s running the DiMeo crime family, dealing with power struggles, betrayals, and the violence that comes with the job. On the other side, he’s trying to be a decent husband to Carmela and a father to Meadow and A.J., even though his work constantly bleeds into his home life. What makes the show especially interesting is that Tony secretly goes to therapy with Dr. Jennifer Melfi, something he hides from everyone in the mob because it would make him look weak.
Through those sessions, you see how much he’s dealing with—anxiety, depression, and a lot of unresolved issues from his childhood, especially his complicated relationship with his mother, Livia. As the series goes on, Tony’s personal and professional worlds collide more and more, and you watch him struggle with who he thinks he is versus the reality of his actions. The show mixes crime, family drama, and psychological depth in a way that made it one of the most influential TV series ever.</body>
